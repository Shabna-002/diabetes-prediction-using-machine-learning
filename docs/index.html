Clinical Biomarker Risk Thresholds

  • Fasting Glucose: <100 mg/dL (Normal), 100–125 (Impaired), ≥126 (Diabetic)
  • Body Mass Index: 18.5–24.9 (Normal), 25–29.9 (Overweight), ≥30 (Obesity)
  • Diastolic BP: <80 mm Hg (Normal), 80–89 (Prehypertension), ≥90 (Hypertension)
  • Pedigree Score: Higher score signifies stronger familial diabetes inheritance risk
  • Age Correlation: Risk accelerates sharply after 35–40 years of age
SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Project Documentation

Overview of the clinical data science methodology, imputation strategy, and validation architecture.

DATASET SPECIFICATIONS

Pima Indians Diabetes

768 clinical observations with 8 physiological risk predictors collected by the National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases.

PREPROCESSING

Median Imputation

Biologically implausible zero values in Glucose, Blood Pressure, Skinfold, Insulin, and BMI are imputed via training set feature medians.

STANDARDIZATION

StandardScaler Normalization

Biomarkers are transformed to zero-mean and unit variance to optimize decision boundaries across linear and non-linear classifiers.
