    SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

    Project Documentation

    Overview of the data science methodology and deployment architecture.

    DATASET

    Pima Indians Diabetes

    768 clinical observations with 8 physiological risk predictors collected by the National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases.

    FEATURE ENGINEERING

    Imputation Strategy

    Zero values in Glucose, Blood Pressure, Skinfold, Insulin, and BMI are treated as biologically invalid and imputed via feature-wise training medians.

    SCALING & EVALUATION

    StandardScaler & Stratification

    Features scaled with zero-mean unit-variance transformation. Evaluated via stratified 80/20 train/test split to preserve positive class balance.
